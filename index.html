// Depth-Based Perception

SPATIAL PERCEPTION

The drone observes the surrounding geometry through depth images. A convolutional autoencoder compresses every frame into a compact latent representation that the navigation policy can process efficiently.

// Mapping & Localization

VISUAL SLAM

The real-time SLAM system turns depth observations from the front, left, and right cameras into a continuously updated 2D occupancy grid. The map lets the drone separate unknown space, safe free space, and walls or obstacles while choosing where to explore next.

SLAM controls exploration and navigation; YOLO runs separately to confirm people and report their positions to the rescue logic.

MAPPING PIPELINE

Depth pixels are projected into world space and written into a probabilistic occupancy grid. Free rays are drawn from the drone to visible surfaces, while surface hits become obstacles or walls. Safety inflation expands blocked areas so planned routes keep clearance.

FRONTIER EXPLORATION

A frontier is a reachable free cell touching unknown space. Candidates are clustered and scored by unknown gain, clearance, direction, blacklist state, visited regions, and corridor preference before the best target is committed.

01

REACHABLE TARGETS

One BFS starts from the drone, so selected frontiers are reachable through the currently observed map.

02

CLEAR A*

Centered A* plans through known-free space, prefers wall clearance, and replans when new map data blocks the path.

03

CORRIDOR LOGIC

Corridor mode favors forward motion and side openings, extends targets deeper, and avoids unnecessary returns.

04

RECOVERY

Probes, blacklist recovery, low-gain recovery, and controlled backtracking keep exploration moving when frontiers fail.

Coverage is measured from known cells against the expected mapped area. The mission finishes only when coverage is high enough and dynamically spawned people have been detected by the separate YOLO rescue pipeline.

// Mission Monitoring

MISSION DASHBOARD

The dashboard is the mission control layer for the 3D Drone RL project. It brings together the Isaac Sim environment, PPO navigation policy, SLAM mapping, camera streams, YOLO rescue detection, and recorded flight telemetry into one operator view.

During a run, the interface makes it possible to follow how the autonomous drone understands the room, avoids obstacles, searches unexplored frontiers, identifies people, and stores every mission frame for later review.

SLAM 3D map, 2D radar, frontiers, goal distance, and explored coverage.
PPO + AE Navigation policy driven by depth perception and compact latent features.
YOLO v11 Person detection with confidence tracking and rescue log confirmation.
Replay JSONL archive review with synchronized mission telemetry and frames.

3D SLAM Map

Live spatial awareness layer showing explored walls, obstacles, drone pose, detected person, goal marker, and a compact 2D radar view.
