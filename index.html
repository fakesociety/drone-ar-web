PROJECT SUMMARY

An autonomous drone capable of navigating complex GPS-denied environments, avoiding obstacles, detecting people in real time, and building spatial maps for search and rescue missions. The system integrates Deep Reinforcement Learning, YOLOv11, and Visual SLAM into a complete perception and navigation pipeline developed in NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab.

TRAINING ENVIRONMENT

Developed using NVIDIA Isaac Sim, a high-fidelity 3D simulator.

Environment Overview

Designed for curriculum training, with progressive stages and randomized obstacle layouts.

Stage 1

A short route with a small obstacle challenge.

Stage 2

An extended route with more obstacles to raise the difficulty.

Stage 3

Varied obstacle types, geometric shapes, and more challenging sizes.

Stage 4

A narrow corridor with horizontal obstacles and a sharp turn.

DEPTH IMAGE COMPRESSION

Raw depth frames contain more information than the RL policy needs and are too large to process efficiently, which would significantly slow down training. The convolutional autoencoder compresses each image into a compact and efficient latent representation while preserving the essential geometric features, obstacle distances, and spatial structure required for navigation.

HIERARCHICAL CONTROL

Navigation and flight stabilization are handled by two dedicated controllers. Both policies were trained with PPO, allowing each controller to specialize in a different level of the task.

HIGH-LEVEL CONTROLLER

HUMAN DETECTION

YOLOv11 processes the camera feed in real time to detect people and return their location and confidence score.

The detector runs as an independent system in parallel with the navigation and flight-control pipeline. It reports human detections without controlling the drone's motion.

VISUAL SLAM

The drone builds a live map as it moves. The real-time SLAM system turns depth observations from the front, left, and right cameras into a continuously updated 2D occupancy grid. The map lets the drone separate unknown space, safe free space, and walls or obstacles while choosing where to explore next.

SENSE

01

Front, left, and right depth cameras capture the surrounding space throughout the flight.

BUILD THE MAP

02

Depth measurements are projected into a probabilistic 2D occupancy grid of free space, walls, obstacles, and unknown areas.

CHOOSE WHERE TO EXPLORE

03

The system detects frontiers (the boundary between mapped and unmapped space) and ranks them by information gain and travel distance.

PLAN, FLY, REPEAT

04

A* generates a clearance-aware path through known-free space. The PPO navigator follows its waypoints while the map and route update continuously.

Live SLAM Mapping

Real-time mapping, localization, and frontier-based exploration.

MISSION DASHBOARD

The dashboard is the mission control layer for the 3D AeroRescue project. It brings together the Isaac Sim environment, PPO navigation policy, SLAM mapping, camera streams, YOLO rescue detection, and recorded flight telemetry into one operator view.

During a run, the interface makes it possible to follow how the autonomous drone understands the room, avoids obstacles, searches unexplored frontiers, identifies people, and stores every mission frame for later review.

3D SLAM Map

Live spatial awareness layer showing explored walls, obstacles, drone pose, detected person, goal marker, and a compact 2D radar view.
